The Story Behind StrongFist

More than a gym — a destination built for strength, discipline, and complete fitness transformation in Khajaguda, Hyderabad.

Our Story

Built For Strength.
Made For You.

StrongFist GYM Health & Fitness opened its doors inside Arka Surabhi on Lanco Hills Road with one goal: give Khajaguda a training space worthy of serious results. Not a crowded box with worn-out equipment — a premium, unisex fitness destination built around real coaching and real accountability.

From muscle gain and fat loss training to nutrition guidance and body recomposition programs, every session here is designed to build confidence, improve performance, and create a stronger lifestyle through dedication and consistency. It shows in the numbers — a 5.0 rating across 46 Google reviews from members who trained here and kept coming back.

★★★★★ 5.0 · 46 Google Reviews

Our Mission

To help every member — beginner or advanced — build real strength, break through plateaus, and become a healthier, more confident version of themselves through personalized coaching, honest programming, and a training environment that pushes you without judgment.

Our Vision

To be Khajaguda's most trusted fitness destination — a unisex, premium gym where strength training, fat loss coaching, nutrition guidance, and transformation programs come together under one roof, one honest rep at a time.
